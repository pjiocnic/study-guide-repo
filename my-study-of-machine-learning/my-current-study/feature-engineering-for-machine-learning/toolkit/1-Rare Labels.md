# Impact of Rare Labels

1. Rare categories can **cause over-fitting**, particularly in **tree-based methods**.
2. Infrequent labels may add noise, which could cause over-fitting.
3. Rare labels may be present only in training set, therefore causing over-fitting.
4. Rare labels may appear only in the test set. Thus, the ML model will not know how to evaluate it.
