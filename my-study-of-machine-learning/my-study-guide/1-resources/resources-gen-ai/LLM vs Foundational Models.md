[Foundation Models Vs LLM(Large Language Models) by Aman Walia](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/foundation-models-vs-llmlarge-language-aman-walia-aslsc/)
- LLMs are a subset of foundation models specifically designed for processing and generating human language
- Foundational models are not limited to language tasks (like in case of LLMs) but can include image recognition, sound processing, and more.
- DALL-E and Alphafold are foundational models that are not LLMs
