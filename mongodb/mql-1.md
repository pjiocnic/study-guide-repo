Here are some solid places to learn MongoDB queries, starting from basics and moving toward more advanced usage.

---

## **1️⃣ Official MongoDB Resources**

* **[MongoDB Manual – CRUD Operations](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/manual/crud/)**
  Step-by-step examples of `find()`, `insert()`, `update()`, `delete()`, plus aggregation basics.
  Good for understanding syntax and options.

* **[MongoDB Aggregation Pipeline Guide](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/manual/aggregation/)**
  Covers `$match`, `$group`, `$project`, `$sort`, `$lookup`, etc.
  Excellent for learning how to build more complex reporting queries.

* **[MongoDB University (Free Courses)](https://learn.mongodb.com/)**
  Recommended free courses:

  * **M001** – MongoDB Basics (perfect starting point)
  * **M121** – Aggregation Framework
  * **M201** – MongoDB Performance
    These are video + hands-on labs using real datasets.

---

## **2️⃣ Interactive Learning**

* **[Mongo Playground](https://mongoplayground.net/)**
  Lets you test MongoDB queries directly in your browser without installing anything.

* **[MongoDB Compass](https://www.mongodb.com/products/compass)**
  GUI tool where you can write queries, see results instantly, and export them.
  You’ve already been using Compass — try the Aggregations tab to learn by doing.

---

## **3️⃣ Books & Cheat Sheets**

* **[MongoDB in Action (Manning)](https://www.manning.com/books/mongodb-in-action-second-edition)** – Great for beginners and intermediate users.
* **[The Little MongoDB Book](https://github.com/karlseguin/the-little-mongodb-book)** – Free and concise introduction.
* **MongoDB Cheat Sheet** – [Cheatography MongoDB](https://cheatography.com/dezren39/cheat-sheets/mongodb/) gives quick command references.

---

## **4️⃣ YouTube Channels**

* **MongoDB Official YouTube** – Has short videos on queries, indexes, and aggregation.
* **Academind** – Has a great beginner’s MongoDB playlist.
* **Traversy Media** – Intro to MongoDB and Mongoose (Node.js ORM) but still teaches raw query concepts.
